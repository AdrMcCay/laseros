LaserOS

Calculators and analysis tools for laser-driven ion acceleration experiments. Pick an app to open it; the address bar link goes straight to that app.

To install LaserOS on this device, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens full screen and works offline.

Thomson Parabola Simulator

Track ions from the pinhole through the magnet and the electric-field plates of a Thomson parabola spectrometer, then see where they land on the detector.

Non-relativistic Euler push · Δt = 10 ps · ≤ 7000 steps
Uniform hard-edged fields · E = V / plate gap · no field outside the E-plates

Settings

Settings files are the same JSON the Python tool reads and writes, so they move between the two.

Spectrometer parameters

E-plates

Pinhole & resolution

Trace width s = d (LC + LT) / LC, with LT the pinhole–detector distance. Energy resolution ΔE/E = 2s / z, with z the magnetic deflection from the zero point.

Detector

Standard slotted CR-39, 50 × 75 mm; the 5 mm slot is for the zero point.
